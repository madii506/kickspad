01The rule

One input. One channel. The alert on their stream is the receipt.

Paste a Kick channel URL. That channel is what the coin is about. The coin's creator fee fills a pot for that channel, and whenever the channel is live and the pot covers a gift pack, Kicks are bought and sent on that stream. The gift alert fires on their screen, in front of their chat, with the coin's name on it.

The send rule is two facts, and both are checkable by anyone watching: the channel is live, and the pot covers a pack. Not a judgement somebody could argue with later. Live, and covered. If either is false, nothing is sent.

The streamer connects nothing, pastes nothing, signs nothing. There is no code for their bio, no login, no claim window. The Kicks land on their stream whether they have heard of the coin or not.

One channel per coin was a decision, not a limitation. A coin that pays five streamers is a coin about nobody. A coin that pays one is about that one. If you want to pay a second streamer, launch a second coin.

03The pot

The pot does not drip. It climbs, and it pays in steps.

Kicks are sold in fixed packs. You cannot send an arbitrary amount; you send a pack, and the pack has a price Kick set. A pot fed by a creator fee grows a little on every trade. A rail that only accepts fixed packs cannot spend that way. So the pot climbs until it covers a rung, and then that rung is sent. When it has climbed high enough to cover a bigger pack, the bigger pack is sent instead. Each rung is an alert on the streamer's screen, and a bigger rung is a bigger alert.

04The receipt

The receipt is the alert on the streamer's own stream.

Every pad in this family has to answer the same question: how do you know the money went where you said? Most answer by drawing something — a receipt card, a payout row, a dashboard they control. A receipt we drew is a receipt we could have drawn differently.

On KICKS the receipt is Kick's alert, on the streamer's screen, in their layout, in front of their chat, at a timestamp in their VOD. It cannot be forged from here, cannot be moved, and cannot be made to say a bigger number than was paid, because the number is Kick's, and Kick is the party being paid.

06The operator

Kick has no API for gifting. A human presses send.

There is no endpoint a program can call to buy a pack and send it to a channel. Gifts are bought and sent by a person, in a Kick account, on a screen. So when a pot covers a pack and the channel is live, a human with a Kick account buys the pack and presses send. This is not described as automated, because it is not.

That human decides nothing. Which channel is fixed by the coin. When is fixed by whether the channel is live. How much is fixed by the pot and Kick's pack sizes. Their role is the one step the rail does not expose to software.

08Status

What is not verifiable today.

Listed here rather than left for you to find. What is checkable right now is the rule, written down, and the docs.

  • mintThere is no mint address. When one exists it will appear on this page and in the pinned post at the same moment. Anyone posting one before that is scamming you.
  • potNo pot has ever bought a Kick. Where a number would go there is a dash, and a dash is not a zero. A zero would claim something was measured and found empty.
  • ledgerThere is no ledger of sends, so there is no ledger on this site. It will be a list of public posts, each with the clip of the alert it refers to.
  • ladderThe rung sizes are not published, on purpose. They will be taken from Kick's own prices at the moment the first pack is bought, and not before.
  • shareKick's share of every gift is not published, on purpose. It will appear when confirmed from Kick's own terms, with a link to the terms.
  • consentNo streamer has consented to any of this. Coins are launched on channels, not with them.
